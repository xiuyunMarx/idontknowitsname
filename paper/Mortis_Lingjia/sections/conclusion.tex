\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We present \sysname, a co-design of the compiler and serving runtime that drives more efficient KV-cache scheduling by exploiting intra-prompt heterogeneity. It uses runtime-assisted static analysis and steers the KV-cache through prompt re-layout and proactive scheduling. Our evaluation shows that \sysname achieves up to 1.99$\times$ JCT speedup and 1.97$\times$ higher throughput over SGLang. \sysname highlights the importance of the structure inside prompts for serving.
