LLM agent frameworks increasingly expose model invocations through structured programming abstractions, with framework runtimes lowering program-level constructs into concrete prompts. We observe that these programmatically generated prompts are inherently exposing their internal heterogeneity boundaries: the different sharing scopes, lifetimes, and evolution patterns of the prompts can be analyzed systematically. However, existing serving systems treat prompts as opaque text, losing this structure and missing opportunities for more effective KV-cache reuse and management. To address the gap, we present \sysname, a compiler-serving co-design that recovers and exploits this heterogeneity for efficient KV-cache management. Our evaluation shows that \sysname achieves up to 1.90$\times$ lower average JCT, 4.49$\times$ lower average TTFT and 1.46$\times$ throughput improvement.
