\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
The exposed intra-prompt heterogeneity of programmatically generated prompts creates opportunities for optimization. \sysname discovers these opportunities by static analysis assisted by runtime observations, and steers KV-cache scheduling with this information. Our evaluation demonstrates that \sysname reduces average JCT by up to 1.90$\times$ and average TTFT by up to 4.49$\times$, and raises throughput by 1.46$\times$ on a mixed workload.
