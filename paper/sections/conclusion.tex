\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
The intra-prompt heterogeneity of programmatically generated prompts creates opportunities for optimization. \sysname discovers these opportunities by static analysis assisted by runtime observations, and steers KV-cache management with this information through adaptive prompt re-layout and proactive KV-cache scheduling. Our evaluation demonstrates that \sysname reduces average JCT by 1.40$\times$ and average TTFT by 1.62$\times$ on geometric mean over vanilla SGLang, and by up to 1.90$\times$ and 4.49$\times$. On a mixed workload, it raises throughput by 1.51$\times$ on geometric mean and by up to 1.86$\times$.
