\section{Implementation}
\label{sec:implementation}
We implemented \sysname on top of Jac byLLM and SGLang. The serving layer of \sysname sits upon model engine and ensure KV-Cache entries are scheduled with repect to their heterogeneity. The compiler side static analysis construct essential metadata from compiler-IR, sending the them to serving layer of \sysname. Necessary modifications of SGLang HiCache engine for \sysname's KV-cache arrangement are also made based on demand.
