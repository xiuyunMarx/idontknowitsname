Tidewater Survey. The Tidewater Survey is a five year monitoring effort that records the shape and sediment load of three estuary channels through the tidal cycle. The survey takes its measurements with the Halcyon spectrometer. It began in 2021 and is administered by the Coastal Dynamics Lab.

Lantern Array Study. The Lantern Array Study characterises the stability of large arrays of coherent light sources over long observation runs. The study takes its measurements with the Perihelion laser bench. It began in 2018 and is administered by the Photonics Lab.

Saltmarsh Census. The Saltmarsh Census produces an annual inventory of vegetation cover and channel geometry across four protected marshes. The census takes its measurements with the Wader drone fleet. It began in 2022 and is administered by the Coastal Dynamics Lab.

Ridgeline Mapping. The Ridgeline Mapping project builds a structural model of a folded mountain belt from surface and subsurface observations. The project takes its measurements with the Orrery seismograph. It began in 2016 and is administered by the Geodynamics Lab.

Prism Calibration Project. The Prism Calibration Project maintains the traceability of the institute's optical measurements against external reference standards. The project takes its measurements with the Halcyon spectrometer. It began in 2021 and is administered by the Photonics Lab.

Windward Sampling. The Windward Sampling programme collects continuous records of aerosol composition on the exposed western coast. The programme takes its measurements with the Zephyr sampler mast. It began in 2019 and is administered by the Atmospheric Chemistry Lab.

Basalt Core Analysis. The Basalt Core Analysis project describes the mineralogy and cooling history of drilled volcanic sequences. The project takes its measurements with the Cutwater core drill. It began in 2018 and is administered by the Geodynamics Lab.

Cirrus Tracking. The Cirrus Tracking project follows the formation and decay of high thin cloud over the institute's field station. The project takes its measurements with the Nimbus lidar. It began in 2023 and is administered by the Atmospheric Chemistry Lab.

Ferrite Aging Study. The Ferrite Aging Study measures how the magnetic properties of ferrite components drift over years of thermal cycling. The study takes its measurements with the Anvil furnace. It began in 2017 and is administered by the Materials Lab.
