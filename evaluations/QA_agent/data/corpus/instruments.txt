Halcyon spectrometer. The Halcyon spectrometer resolves the composition of suspended and dissolved material from reflected light. It is housed in Brackwater Hall. The instrument is maintained by the Optics Support Team and was commissioned in 2015.

Perihelion laser bench. The Perihelion laser bench provides a vibration isolated platform for long baseline interferometry. It is housed in Vaughn Tower. The instrument is maintained by the Optics Support Team and was commissioned in 2013.

Wader drone fleet. The Wader drone fleet consists of six fixed wing aircraft configured for low altitude multispectral survey. It is housed in Thorne Pavilion. The fleet is maintained by the Field Robotics Crew and was commissioned in 2020.

Orrery seismograph. The Orrery seismograph is a broadband three component installation set on a bedrock pier. It is housed in the Kessler Annex. The instrument is maintained by the Geophysics Maintenance Group and was commissioned in 2009.

Zephyr sampler mast. The Zephyr sampler mast carries filter heads and inlets at four heights above the ground for aerosol collection. It is housed in the Marlowe Building. The mast is maintained by the Field Robotics Crew and was commissioned in 2017.

Cutwater core drill. The Cutwater core drill recovers continuous rock cores to a depth of four hundred metres. It is housed in the Kessler Annex. The drill is maintained by the Geophysics Maintenance Group and was commissioned in 2012.

Nimbus lidar. The Nimbus lidar measures backscatter profiles through the full depth of the troposphere. It is housed in the Marlowe Building. The instrument is maintained by the Atmospheric Instruments Team and was commissioned in 2021.

Anvil furnace. The Anvil furnace holds specimens at controlled temperature for thermal cycling experiments lasting many months. It is housed in Brackwater Hall. The furnace is maintained by the Foundry Maintenance Team and was commissioned in 2011.
