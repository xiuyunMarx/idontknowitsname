Coastal Dynamics Lab. The Coastal Dynamics Lab studies the movement of sediment and water along shorelines and in estuaries. It occupies the second and third floors of Thorne Pavilion. The lab is directed by Dr. Ingrid Sollberg, and its research is financed by the Blue Horizon Program.

Photonics Lab. The Photonics Lab develops and maintains optical measurement techniques for use across the institute. It is located in Vaughn Tower. The lab is directed by Dr. Casper Lindqvist, and its research is financed by the Meridian Optics Grant.

Geodynamics Lab. The Geodynamics Lab investigates the deformation of the crust and the composition of deep rock. It is located in the Kessler Annex. The lab is directed by Dr. Yusuf Adeyemi, and its research is financed by the Deep Earth Initiative.

Atmospheric Chemistry Lab. The Atmospheric Chemistry Lab measures the composition of the lower and middle atmosphere and the particles suspended in it. It occupies the west wing of the Marlowe Building. The lab is directed by Dr. Priya Ramanathan, and its research is financed by the Skyward Consortium.

Materials Lab. The Materials Lab examines how engineered materials change under heat, stress and prolonged use. It is located in Brackwater Hall. The lab is directed by Dr. Rosalind Achterberg, and its research is financed by the Foundry Alliance.

Instrumentation Lab. The Instrumentation Lab designs bespoke hardware and control electronics for the institute's other laboratories. It occupies the ground floor of Thorne Pavilion. The lab is directed by Dr. Milos Petrovic, and its research is financed by the Foundry Alliance.
