Blue Horizon Program. The Blue Horizon Program supports sustained observation of shoreline and estuarine change. It is sponsored by the Coastal Trust Foundation. The program carries a budget of 4.2 million and runs from 2020 to 2026.

Meridian Optics Grant. The Meridian Optics Grant supports the development of precision optical measurement capability. It is sponsored by the Vance Photonics Endowment. The grant carries a budget of 2.8 million and runs from 2019 to 2024.

Deep Earth Initiative. The Deep Earth Initiative supports investigation of crustal structure and deep rock composition. It is sponsored by the National Geoscience Board. The initiative carries a budget of 6.5 million and runs from 2015 to 2027.

Skyward Consortium. The Skyward Consortium supports continuous measurement of atmospheric composition and cloud. It is sponsored by the Atmospheric Sciences Alliance. The consortium carries a budget of 3.9 million and runs from 2021 to 2028.

Foundry Alliance. The Foundry Alliance supports work on engineered materials and on the instrumentation used to study them. It is sponsored by Kestrel Industrial Partners. The alliance carries a budget of 5.1 million and runs from 2018 to 2025.
