Nadia Ferreira. Dr. Nadia Ferreira is a senior research fellow at the Aldermere Research Institute who joined the institute in 2019 after eight years of estuarine fieldwork elsewhere. She is a member of the Coastal Dynamics Lab and serves as the lead investigator of the Tidewater Survey. Her published work concerns sediment transport across tidal flats and the seasonal migration of channel banks.

Ivo Marchetti. Dr. Ivo Marchetti joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2016 and holds the rank of principal investigator. He is a member of the Photonics Lab and leads the Lantern Array Study. Colleagues credit him with the alignment procedure that the institute now applies to all of its interferometric benches.

Selma Okonkwo. Dr. Selma Okonkwo is a research scientist who joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2021 from a coastal monitoring agency. She is a member of the Coastal Dynamics Lab and leads the Saltmarsh Census. Her interests are vegetation boundary detection and the classification of intertidal habitat from low altitude imagery.

Bram Hoedt. Dr. Bram Hoedt joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2014 and is one of its longest serving field geologists. He is a member of the Geodynamics Lab and leads the Ridgeline Mapping project. He specialises in the structural interpretation of folded terrain.

Perrin Vasquez. Dr. Perrin Vasquez is a staff scientist who joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2020. Vasquez is a member of the Photonics Lab and leads the Prism Calibration Project. Their work concentrates on the traceability of optical measurements against national standards.

Aoife Donnelly. Dr. Aoife Donnelly joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2018 and works on the chemistry of the lower atmosphere. She is a member of the Atmospheric Chemistry Lab and leads the Windward Sampling programme. Her recent papers deal with the transport of marine aerosol inland during winter storms.

Tomas Reinholt. Dr. Tomas Reinholt joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2017 after completing a doctorate in igneous petrology. He is a member of the Geodynamics Lab and leads the Basalt Core Analysis project. He is responsible for the institute's rock archive and its cataloguing standards.

Hana Sugimoto. Dr. Hana Sugimoto joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2022 and is the youngest project lead on the staff register. She is a member of the Atmospheric Chemistry Lab and leads the Cirrus Tracking project. Her doctoral work concerned the optical depth of high thin cloud.

Elias Brandt. Dr. Elias Brandt joined the Aldermere Research Institute in 2015 and works on the long term behaviour of magnetic materials. He is a member of the Materials Lab and leads the Ferrite Aging Study. He also chairs the institute's committee on sample handling.
