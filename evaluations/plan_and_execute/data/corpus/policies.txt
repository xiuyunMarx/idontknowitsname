Archive retention policy. The institute archive retains material on a schedule that distinguishes raw instrument series from derived products. Raw series are retained for ten years from deposit and may not be deleted before that term without the written agreement of the depositing investigator; derived products are retained for five years and are deleted automatically at term unless renewed. Every deposit must arrive with an instrument quality report attached, a requirement introduced in 2024 after the Halcyon spectrometer's calibration drift showed that silently corrupted data had been accepted for months, and the archive will reject a deposit that lacks one. Rejection is not a soft state: for efforts financed by the Blue Horizon Program a rejected deposit does not count toward the quarterly reimbursement claim, so a paperwork failure becomes a cash flow failure within one quarter. The Tidewater Survey holds the only standing exemption to the ten year rule, negotiated by Dr. Nadia Ferreira, which allows its raw transect series to be retained indefinitely on the grounds that the channel migration record has no natural expiry; the exemption applies to the raw series only and not to the derived atlas, which is deleted and renewed on the ordinary five year cycle. Investigators are reminded that the exemption does not relieve the survey of the quality report requirement, and that the archive's rejection log, not the investigator's own records, is the authoritative account of what has been deposited.

Instrument downtime policy. Scheduled maintenance is booked against the instrument calendar a quarter in advance and is not charged to the group whose time it displaces. Unscheduled downtime is charged to no one, but the displaced group has no claim on recovery time beyond the next available booking, which for the Halcyon spectrometer in field season means a wait of about two weeks. Groups may not swap bookings privately; all changes pass through the instrument scheduling board on which each lab holds one seat. The policy exists because the recovery of tidal cycle measurements is time bound in a way that laboratory measurements are not, and the board is expected to weight that asymmetry when it settles a conflict.

Data release and embargo policy. Derived products are released on publication. Raw series carry a default embargo of twelve months from deposit, extendable once by application. The Lantern Array Study applies the full twelve months to its interferograms; the Tidewater Survey applies no embargo at all to its transect series, having released it openly since 2021, which is why outside requests for institute data are dominated by that one series.
