Coastal Dynamics Lab. The Coastal Dynamics Lab studies the movement of sediment and water along shorelines and in estuaries. It occupies the second and third floors of Thorne Pavilion, is directed by Dr. Ingrid Sollberg, and is financed by the Blue Horizon Program. It administers two efforts, the Tidewater Survey and the Saltmarsh Census, and allocates a single field crew of four between them, which is why the two compete for late summer time. The lab holds one seat on the instrument scheduling board and uses it primarily to defend the Tidewater Survey's Halcyon spectrometer bookings, since tidal cycle measurements cannot be recovered outside their window. Its standing weakness, acknowledged in each of its last two annual reports, is the archive deposit backlog that followed the 2024 reprocessing.

Photonics Lab. The Photonics Lab develops and maintains optical measurement techniques for use across the institute. It is located in Vaughn Tower, is directed by Dr. Casper Lindqvist, and is financed by the Meridian Optics Grant. It administers the Lantern Array Study and owns the Perihelion laser bench, on which it sells time to other groups at an internal rate. Unlike the Coastal Dynamics Lab it carries no field crew, and its scheduling conflicts are between internal and external users of one bench rather than between two efforts of its own.
